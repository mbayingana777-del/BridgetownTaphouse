Wings / Chicken Favorites

🥨 Starters

  • Pretzel Sticks
    Warm, soft pretzels with rich beer cheese for dipping — salty, buttery, and impossible to ignore.
  • Beer-Battered Pickles
    Thick-cut pickles in a crisp beer batter — crunchy outside, tangy inside, gone fast.
  • Fries or Tots
    Hot, golden, and perfectly crisp — made for sharing, but no judgment if you don’t.

🥗 Salads

  • Bridgetown Salad
    Fresh greens, crisp toppings, and house flavor — light, refreshing, and surprisingly filling.
  • Buffalo Grilled Chicken Salad
    Grilled chicken with Buffalo heat over fresh greens — bold flavor with a clean finish.

🍔 Burgers

All burgers served with your choice of fries or tots.
  • Bridgetown Burger ★
    Bacon, fried egg, roasted Anaheim pepper, Muenster, sautéed onion, and house sauce — stacked, rich, and unforgettable.
  • Pittsburgher ★
    Capicola, fried egg, provolone, and slaw — big flavor, local pride, and pure satisfaction.
  • Velvet Elvis ★
    Peanut butter, bacon, banana, and American cheese — sweet, salty, and shockingly perfect.
  • The Animal ★
    Double meat, double cheese, fully loaded — bold, indulgent, and built to impress.

🍗 Grilled Chicken

  • Bruschetta Grilled Chicken
    Grilled chicken topped with fresh mozzarella, bright bruschetta, and balsamic glaze — fresh, savory, and crave-worthy.
  • La Flama Blanca
    Buffalo heat, bacon, ghost pepper, habanero jack, and jalapeño — fiery, fearless, and not holding back.

🍗 Wings

  • Traditional Wings ★
    Crispy, juicy wings tossed your way — classic flavors that keep the table buzzing.
  • Boneless Wings
    Tender, bite-sized, and sauced up — all the flavor with easy bites.
