Menu

Simple, classic favorites done right. Ask your server about daily specials.

Starters

Great to share.
  • Edamame
    Warm, lightly salted edamame — simple, clean, and addictive.
  • Fries or Tots
    Crispy golden fries or tots, served hot and ready for dipping.
  • Chili Fries or Tots
    Loaded with hearty chili and melted cheese — comfort food done right.
  • Fresh Mozzarella
    Tomato, basil, balsamic glaze — fresh, bright, and classic.
  • Scotch Eggs
    A pub favorite — golden and hearty, perfect with a cold drink.
  • Beer-Battered Pickles
    Crunchy beer-battered pickles with that tangy snap in every bite.

Soups & Salads

Dressings include ranch, bleu cheese, balsamic vinaigrette, creamy Italian, oil & vinegar, honey mustard.
  • Soup of the Day
    Ask what’s simmering today — always warm, fresh, and made to hit the spot.
  • Turkey Chili
    Rich and hearty with a lighter twist — big flavor, perfect warmth.
  • House Salad
    Crisp greens and fresh toppings — simple, refreshing, and clean.
  • Chicken Salad
    Tender chicken over fresh greens — protein-packed and satisfying.
  • Steak Salad
    Juicy steak over crisp greens — bold, filling, and built like a meal.
  • Buffalo Grilled Chicken Salad
    Grilled chicken with Buffalo heat — a spicy, fresh classic.

Burgers

Choice of beef, turkey, or portobella.
  • Bridgetown Burger ★
    Bacon, fried egg, roasted Anaheim pepper, Muenster, sautéed onion, burger sauce.
  • Classic Burger
    American cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion — the all-time classic done right.
  • Portobella & Swiss
    Portobella cap, sautéed onion, Swiss — savory, melty, and satisfying.
  • Black & Bleu
    Bacon, gorgonzola, Cajun seasoning — smoky, bold, and creamy with a kick.
  • Pittsburgher ★
    Capicola, fried egg, provolone, slaw — the Pittsburgh move, stacked and legendary.
  • BBQ Burger
    House braised pulled pork, bacon, cheddar, red onion, chipotle BBQ — sweet heat perfection.
  • Velvet Elvis ★
    Bacon, fried egg, peanut butter, American cheese, mayo, pickle — salty/sweet magic.
  • The Animal ★
    American cheese, lettuce, tomato, pickle, sautéed onion, Thousand Island — messy in the best way.

  • BLT + Egg

  • Hippie

Grilled Chicken
